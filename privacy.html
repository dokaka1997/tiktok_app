1. Who we are

ToolHay (the "Service") is a private, self-hosted content publishing tool operated by [[OPERATOR_NAME]] (the "Operator", "we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Service handles, why, and what choices you have. Contact: [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

In short: the Service stores only what it needs to publish your own videos to your own social media accounts. It does not sell data, does not share data with advertisers or data brokers, does not build advertising profiles, and does not read your private messages, followers or contacts.

3. How we use the data

Data is used strictly to operate the Service, specifically to:

  • authenticate you and the social accounts you connect;
  • upload and publish the videos you select, at the times you schedule;
  • show publishing status, history and errors;
  • report back statistics on posts the Service published;
  • diagnose failures through application logs.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making with legal effects, or machine-learning training.

4. How we share data

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. Data leaves the Service only in these cases:

  • The destination platform — when you publish, the video file and its title/description/tags are transmitted to that platform (for example TikTok) so it can be posted to your account. This is the purpose of the Service.
  • Legal obligation — where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

Content published to a social platform is then governed by that platform's own privacy policy, including the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

The Service is self-hosted: data is stored in a database and file storage controlled by the Operator, located in [[HOSTING_LOCATION]]. Protective measures include:

  • access tokens are encrypted at rest before being written to the database;
  • the administrative interface requires authentication;
  • access is limited to the Operator and explicitly authorised personnel;
  • traffic to third-party platform APIs uses HTTPS.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the scale of the Service.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect at any time. Removing a connected account in the Service deletes its stored tokens and stops all future publishing to it.
  • Revoke from the platform. You can also revoke the Service's access directly in your TikTok, YouTube or Facebook account settings, which takes effect immediately.
  • Access, correct or delete. You may request a copy of the data the Service holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Publishing a video necessarily transmits it to the destination platform, which may process and store it on servers outside your country under that platform's own safeguards.
